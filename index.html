Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about TechFlow

Is TechFlow secure?

+

Yes, TechFlow uses enterprise-grade security measures including end-to-end encryption, regular security audits, and compliance with industry standards.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

+

Absolutely! You can cancel your subscription at any time with no cancellation fees. Your data will remain accessible until the end of your billing period.

Do you offer a free trial?

+

Yes, we offer a 14-day free trial for all plans. No credit card required to get started.

What kind of support do you provide?

+

We provide 24/7 customer support for Enterprise plans, priority support for Professional plans, and email support for Starter plans.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?

+

Yes, you can change your plan at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately, while downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.
